💡 Key Findings

  • EALLIS outperforms both baselines across all detection and segmentation metrics.
  • The largest improvement is in Box mAP@50 (+9.6% over ISiD), indicating better object localization even at lenient thresholds.
  • At the strict mAP@75 threshold, EALLIS still maintains strong gains, demonstrating higher quality boundary predictions.
  • The adaptive illumination module and edge-aware loss contribute to consistent improvements across all scales.
  • Small object performance remains challenging (mAP_s ≈ 0%) for all models — this is expected given the extreme low-light degradation at small scales.
